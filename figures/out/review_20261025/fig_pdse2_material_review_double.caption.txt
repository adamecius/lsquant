FIG. 2. PDSE2 clean SIESTA material review. Solid gray denotes the full Hamiltonian and operators; dashed blue denotes the retained spectral subspace of the implemented shift projection, with absolute window [-5,5] eV. (a) Bands on the indicated reciprocal-space path, main view within 3 eV of the reference; the inset contains the full original energy range. Retained bands overlap the gray curves. (b) Exact Gaussian DOS per cell with projected identity, excluding shifted complement states. (c) Exact finite-width Kubo-Greenwood longitudinal conductivity, including intraband terms. (d) Exact spin Hall response with Pauli spin, in e^2/h; the inset zooms into the gap and includes the dotted-orange saved projected M=1024, R=16, seed=12345, Jackson 10 meV KPM result, which uses a different kernel. Band energies and response chemical potentials are measured from mu_ref=1.052 eV relative to stored SIESTA Fermi zero; for PdSe2 this selects the previously studied upper gap. (e,f) Spin texture of the highest occupied valence band below mu_ref, original and explicitly projected, respectively. Where energy-degenerate, the unique maximal eigenstate of physical sigma_z within the multiplet is selected; an unresolved spin eigenspace is left blank. Blue-to-orange fill encodes sigma_z from -1 to +1; black arrows encode (sigma_x,sigma_y), with one Pauli unit equal to 0.125 inverse Angstrom on the wave-vector axes. The black outline is the first Brillouin zone. Degenerate spin-resolved states depend on the stated spin quantisation convention, not an arbitrary energy eigenvector. The retained projected spin agrees with the original within the error in the JSON receipt. Parameters: 24x24 Gamma-centred mesh, W=0, exact Lowdin metric, covariant physical velocity, Gaussian standard deviations 50 meV for DOS/longitudinal conductivity and 10 meV for the spin Hall occupation. The charge curve depends on artificial width and mesh and is not a converged DC transport result. This is a review figure, not a stage-closure certificate.
