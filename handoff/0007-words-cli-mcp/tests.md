  (`~/s7logs/aron_rel.log`):
  - `.chebmom1D`: 3.1e-16 to 6.9e-16;
  - `.chebmom2D`: 2.3e-15 to 3.4e-15;

  Chebyshev step. The generalised route (exact `S^-1`), the Löwdin route with the Chebyshev applier,
  and every orthogonal path are byte-identical between 1 and 16 threads.
- This agrees with the stage-6 tester (06384ce): "T=1 vs T=16: 4 lowdin/exact rows differ at

compared there.
