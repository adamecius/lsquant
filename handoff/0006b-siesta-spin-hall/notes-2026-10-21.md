## 2. Defects found in the new code

1. **Stage-3 reconstruction energy axis** (`python/lsquant/reconstruct.py`, five Kubo
   formulas): `energies = x/scale + shift` where `shift` is the dimensionless rescale shift,
   not the centre; wrong for every non-centred window, invisible to the goldens because
   all stage-0 windows are centred. Fix = `(x - shift)/scale`. Assigned: handoff 0004
   review 3 (Agi); the tester adds an off-centre golden (G4c, graphene, BOUNDS -3.5 2.5)
   at the stage-4 closure. `dos_curve` was already right.
2. **ISCHO applier is serial**: the PCG triangular solves in `IschoInverseOverlap` run on
   one core; at 64 800 orbitals the generalised route took more than 2 h for M = 1024 and
   16 vectors. Stage 8 item (parallel preconditioner or a better S^-1 applier).
3. **Spectral width of SIESTA bases**: Bi DZP spectrum 81 eV, PdSe2 187 eV (semicore).
   KPM resolution at M = 1024 is 0.25 and 0.5 eV. Either M of several thousand, or a
   smaller basis, or a spectral filter. To be decided in 0006b.
4. `stage6_nonorth` (dd9e908 binary) has `ExactInverseOverlap` capped at 4096 and no
   `--window gershgorin`; the k-block operators needed explicit windows from the exact
   spectrum. The review-1 version prints applier residuals (not used here).

## 3. Pipeline pitfalls (SIESTA, Wannier, wannier2sparse)

1. **SIESTA stores H with E_F = 0** in TSHS/HSX; `read_fermi_level()` returns the printed
   E_F (-3.06 eV for Bi), which must not be subtracted again. Found because the Bi gap sat
   at [-0.13, +0.17] eV in the stored H while the "E_F-relative" DOS showed a metal.
2. **SIESTA band-lines block hangs** in the conda MPI build after SCF (`BandLines` with
   `Diag.ParallelOverK`); the HSX/TSHS were already written, so the run was killed and
   used.
3. **wannier2sparse label-route spin current is zero** for SOC Wannier functions without
   `_s+_/_s-_` tags; `JXSZ.CSR` came out with nnz = 0. Build J = 1/2 {VX, SZexact} from
   the exact spin instead (done in Python, 24 M nonzeros, Hermitian to 4e-17).
4. **pw2wannier90 must write the formatted `.spn`** (`spn_formatted = .true.`);
   wannier2sparse asserts on the binary one (`read_spn: nb == num_bands`).
5. **Wannier90 writes `<seed>_centres.xyz` with atoms included**; wannier2sparse wants
   `<seed>.xyz` with the 12 centres only (`assert_equal 18 != 12`) and a `<seed>.uc`
   that Wannier90 does not write (take it from `unit_cell_cart`).
6. **Wannierisation quality**: with the tutorial's 10/10 iterations the spreads are
   7.6-8.5 A^2; with 300/500 iterations 5.8-9.8 A^2. The 12-band model has no clean gap in
   the conduction manifold at 1.2 eV in these runs, so the tutorial's high-energy
   non-trivial phase is not reproduced; the user attributes this to the velocity
   (interband matrix elements) rather than to the gap; to be revisited with the covariant
   velocity (`_r.dat` was produced) and with the Berry-connection term of theory.md eq. 4.
7. **Gershgorin windows on Wannier Hamiltonians** are 4x wider than the spectrum
   (spread-out hoppings): use the eigenvalue range.
8. **Killed process**: the first Bi M = 1024 run died after 2 h without output (no error,
   `set -e` ended the script); cause not found (not the stage-6 tester). Relaunched.
9. **A running bash script must not be overwritten** (bash reads it incrementally): the
   PdSe2 pipeline died with a syntax error when `run_shc.sh` was replaced under it.
10. **pkill patterns match the ssh shell that launches them** (`pkill -f run_x.sh` kills
    the caller); use `[x]` bracket tricks or exact process names.

The three `stage6_nonorth` generalised-route runs (Bi 30x30 k, M = 1024 and M = 4096;
PdSe2 12x12 k, M = 2048) were stopped after 6 to 7 h without output. Estimate: with
`cond(S) ~ 1e3` to `3.5e3` the ISCHO-preconditioned CG at `tol = 1e-10` needs of the
order of 100 iterations per solve, one solve per recursion step on each side, i.e.
`2 M N_states` solves of `~1e7` flops each running on one core: tens of hours. The
exact non-orthogonal references (section 1) stand; the KPM demonstration on a real
SIESTA basis waits for the stage-8 applier work (parallel triangular solves or a
Chebyshev `S^-1` with a good preconditioner, and a looser, controlled tolerance).

## 7. Decision (user, 2026-10-21 10:30): S^-1 exact first, then a study of its expansion

For the real-material route `S^-1` is applied **exactly** (direct factorisation: dense
`LLT` per k block in the Bloch representation; sparse direct Cholesky, `Eigen::SimplicialLLT`
or CHOLMOD, for real-space `S`), not by an iterative or truncated applier. A KPM word on a
non-orthogonal basis is a nested expansion (every step of the outer Chebyshev recursion
applies `S^-1`), so the expansion of `S^-1` gets its own study before it is allowed in a
production path: a handoff ("0006c, the S^-1 expansion") with these experiments on the
rotated fixtures (cond 5 to 7), a cond ~100 fixture and the real Bi `S` (cond ~3500):
(a) Chebyshev coefficient decay of `1/x` on `[lambda_min, lambda_max]` vs the condition
number, against `n ~ (sqrt(kappa)/2) ln(1/eps)`; (b) the error of the truncated `S^-1`
propagated into the DOS and Kubo moments as a function of the order and of the moment
index (the non-Hermitian perturbation of `S^-1 H`); (c) sensitivity to the interval padding,
asymmetric (an eigenvalue below `a` diverges); (d) Chebyshev on the preconditioned operator
`M^-1 S` (block Jacobi, incomplete Cholesky, sparse approximate inverse) and its cost in
parallel SpMVs; (e) the total cost model `M x order` against the exact factorisation;
(f) which combination reproduces the exact-`S^-1` goldens to 1e-12 and at what price.
Byte-level goldens of the non-orthogonal word are generated with the exact `S^-1`.

Refinement (user, same conversation): `S^-1` is a property of the basis, not of the word,
the energy, the random vector or the on-site disorder, so it is built **once** per basis
(per structure sample if the disorder is structural) and the engine then sees
`X = S^-1` as an ordinary operator: `X H`, `X V`. No expansion is nested in the recursion
at run time; the study of 0006c is about how to build `X` once and how to certify it once
(`||S X - 1||`, Hermiticity of `X`):
- Bloch representation: `X(k)` dense per block, `O(N_k W^3)` once, `O(W^2)` per application
  (what `ExactInverseOverlap` already does on the block fixtures);
- real space: sparse direct Cholesky once, then either the triangular solves per application
  or, better, an explicit sparse `X`: `S^-1` of a well-conditioned positive-definite sparse
  `S` decays exponentially with distance (Benzi-Golub), so `X` truncated at a cutoff
  radius is sparse, Hermitian by construction (symmetrise), built once by applying the
  Chebyshev `1/x` series (or the factorisation) to the identity columns in parallel, and
  certified by the residual before any word runs; its range grows with `sqrt(kappa)`, which
  is the one-off price of a redundant basis;
- the applied error is then a fixed Hermitian perturbation of `S^-1 H`, measured once, not a
  per-step iterative tolerance.

## 9. Addendum (2026-10-21, 20:30 UTC, Aron): the covariant velocity of wannier2sparse is not Hermitian

Operators regenerated on Aron from the same PdSe2 Wannier files (`~/from_fatnode/pdse2/ex`,
`out_<mode>/`), Hermiticity measured on the raw CSR files (no fold):

| mode | VX nnz | max abs VX | max abs (VX - VX^+) | relative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bare | 5 550 000 | 6.125 | 0 | 0 |
| berry_connection (the 2026-10-20 run) | 5 550 000 | 6.125 | 0 | 0 |
| covariant | 29 150 000 | 1.19 | 0.456 | 0.38 (VY: 0.61) |

`HAM` and `SZexact` are Hermitian in every mode. So the `covariant` mode of wannier2sparse
(`-i R H - i[H,A]` with `A` from `_r.dat`) produces a non-Hermitian velocity: a defect in
its construction (one-sided commutator, or `A(R)` not folded/conjugated consistently over
the Wigner-Seitz images), not a property of the physics. Note also that `bare` and
`berry_connection` give the same nnz and the same max element here: the Wannier centres
enter only the phases, which for a supercell CSR means the same sparsity.

Consequences:
- The KPM run of the covariant mode is meaningless until this is fixed: lsquant's
  `ReadCsrText` (and the Python `read_csr_text` default) **silently fold every operator to
  `1/2 (M + M^+)`** (legacy behaviour kept for byte identity), so a wrong, non-Hermitian
  velocity enters the engine as its Hermitian part without any warning. Candidate for
  AGENTS.md section 10 and for the clean-up: report the anti-Hermitian norm and refuse
  above a tolerance instead of folding silently.
- wannier2sparse also writes an empty spin current (`JXSZ`, nnz 0) in the `covariant` and
  `bare` modes (the 2026-10-20 pitfall again); `J = 1/2 {VX, SZ}` was rebuilt from the CSR
  files on Aron (`build_j.py`).
- The user's remark (2026-10-21): the velocity operator has to be reconstructed correctly,
  on both routes; the SIESTA derivation is the suspect for the Bi/PdSe2 SIESTA results, and
  the Wannier `covariant` derivation is now shown to be wrong as implemented. The 6b
  self-check (rotated Kane-Mele with `A' = T^+ R T`, Hermiticity of every mode) is the test
  that pins the formula before any material is run again.

## 11. Addendum (2026-10-21, 22:00 UTC, Aron): where the non-Hermitian velocity comes from, both routes

**Wannier route (PdSe2, `_r.dat` from Wannier90 on the 4 x 4 x 1 grid).** Diagnostics
`diag_rdat.py`, `diag_rdat2.py` (scratchpad, run on Aron):
- `H(R)` from `_hr.dat` is exactly Hermitian as an operator (`H(R) = H(-R)^+`).
- The position matrix `A_a(R) = <0n|r_a|Rm>` is **not**: `max|A(R) - A(-R)^+|` = 0.14 A (x),
  0.38 A (y), 0 (z), i.e. 2.5 % and 6.4 % of max|A|. Wannier90 builds it by finite
  differences of the overlaps on the k grid, so on a 4 x 4 grid it is only approximately
  Hermitian. The worst R are Wigner-Seitz boundary vectors.
- `_r.dat` carries no `ndegen` block; `create_hopping_list` divides `H` by `ndegen` (16 of
  the 25 R vectors have ndegen 2 or 4) but `readPositionMatrix` does not divide `A`
  (documented as a pitfall in `tbmodel.hpp`, not handled).
- Covariant velocity rebuilt in k space, `V = dH_bare - i [H(k), A(k)]`, at 8 random k:
  raw `A`: anti-Hermitian part 5 % (x), 22 % (y) of max|V|; `A/ndegen`: 8 %, 10 %;
  `A` symmetrised (`A(R) <- 1/2 [A(R) + A(-R)^+]`) **and** divided by ndegen: 3e-16, 5e-16.
  So the fix is mechanical: hermitise and ndegen-normalise `A` before the commutator.
  The supercell CSR of wannier2sparse does neither (its VX covariant had 38 % anti-Hermitian
  part). Separate oddity: `diag A(0)` differs from `_centres.xyz` by +-6.169 A in z for
  some orbitals (an image/wrapping convention), irrelevant for v_x, v_y.
- Even with a Hermitian `A`, the quality of the Berry connection from a 4 x 4 grid is poor;
  the PdSe2 model should be re-Wannierised on a denser NSCF grid before any plateau claim.

**SIESTA route (Bi(111) bilayer, `bi.TSHS`, sisl 0.16.4, atomic gauge).** Diagnostic
`diag_siesta.py` at 3 random k, directions x and y:
- `H(k)`, `S(k)`, `dH`, `dS` Hermitian to 1e-8 (TSHS round-off); the eq. (4) velocity
  `V = dH - 1/2 (dS S^-1 H + H S^-1 dS)` Hermitian to 2e-8, max|V| 10 to 16 eV A.
- Hellmann-Feynman: `c_n^+ V c_n = dE_n/dk_a` (central differences of the generalised
  eigenvalues, delta = 1e-4 1/A) to 1e-5 against values of order 5; passes.
- The legacy one-sided velocity `dH - dS S^-1 H` is non-Hermitian by 19 to 33 eV A (larger
  than max|V| itself) while its diagonal passes the same Hellmann-Feynman test: exactly
  what theory.md section 3 says, intraband right, interband wrong by `1/2 (E_n - E_m) <n|dS|m>`.
  This is the quantity to put in front of the claim "the legacy implementation is fine":
  for sigma_xx and bands it is; for sigma_xy it is not Hermitian, and the Bi numbers
  (0.97 new vs 1.05 legacy, both near 1) say the correction is a few percent there.
- What the SIESTA velocity still lacks is the intra-cell dipole term (the analogue of the
  off-diagonal `A`), which no diagnostic on `H`, `S` alone can test; the rotated Kane-Mele
  self-check of `brief.md` section 8 is the test for it.
